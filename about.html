MY STORY

Ambitious by nature. Committed to growth.

My name is Thebe Toteng, and I am a first-year Business Intelligence and Data Analytics student at Botswana Accountancy College. I am an enthusiastic and hardworking individual who is interested in understanding how information can be transformed into knowledge, insight and practical solutions.

I have a particular interest in statistics and business-related subjects because they encourage logical reasoning, critical thinking and evidence-based decision-making. I am especially interested in how these disciplines can be applied within the financial industry.

I describe myself as charismatic, enthusiastic, hardworking and elegant. I also value perseverance and adaptability, qualities that I believe are important when learning unfamiliar concepts, responding to challenges and pursuing long-term goals.

At this stage of my journey, I am focused on developing my knowledge, discovering my strengths and laying a solid foundation for my future career. I see education as an opportunity to grow intellectually, professionally and personally.
