THE PROFESSIONAL STANDARD

My email communication checklist

Before sending an important email, I can use this checklist to ensure that my message is clear, respectful, and complete.

  • Use a clear and specific subject line.
  • Address the recipient appropriately.
  • Introduce myself and explain the purpose of the message.
  • Keep the message concise, relevant, and logically organised.
  • Use respectful language and correct spelling and grammar.
  • Include relevant attachments when requested and refer to them in the message.
  • Check the recipient's email address before sending.
  • End with a professional closing and accurate contact details.
  • Review the message before clicking Send.

MY DIGITAL PROFESSIONALISM

Every message represents my professional identity.

Professional email communication helps establish trust, communicate intentions clearly, and build positive academic and workplace relationships. I aim to make every message purposeful, respectful, and appropriate for its intended audience.
